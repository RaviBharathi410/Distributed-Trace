| 2026-09-27 | Senior Product & Engineering Partner | Strict RBAC route mounting (`RequireRole`) and CORS allowlist validation | Allow all authenticated users to manage keys/resolve anomalies; wildcard CORS | Principle of least privilege: API key creation/revocation is restricted to `Admin` and `Owner`; anomaly status mutation is restricted to `Member`, `Admin`, and `Owner` (`ReadOnly`/`Viewer` cannot alter state).
